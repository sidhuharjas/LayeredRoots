About Layered Roots

At Layered Roots, we celebrate the beauty of heritage, the strength of community, and the stories that connect generations.

Inspired by Sikh values, Gurmukhi, and our shared traditions, we create meaningful products that help keep our roots alive in everyday life. Each piece is thoughtfully designed to inspire learning, preserve culture, and create connections.

From Gurmukhi learning tools and Sikh-inspired keepsakes to personalized creations made with love, our work is more than just a product — it is a way to celebrate identity, share knowledge, and pass our traditions forward.

By blending modern design with timeless values, Layered Roots brings heritage into homes, classrooms, and communities in new and creative ways.

Rooted in heritage. Crafted with purpose. Created to inspire future generations.

Our Story

Layered Roots was created from a simple idea: the things we surround ourselves with should have meaning.

We love turning ideas into lasting keepsakes — pieces that celebrate people, milestones, traditions, and everyday moments. Every order is made with care, one layer at a time, because we believe the little details matter.

Thank you for supporting a small business that creates with purpose. We're grateful to be part of your story.
